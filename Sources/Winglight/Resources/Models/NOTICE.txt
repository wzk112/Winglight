reference. Their diagrams are not bundled or represented as our artwork.

of their respective owners; this is an independent simulation, not an official
or endorsed airline/manufacturer application.

A320 Air China 8192 x 8192 atlas and editable XCF source:
https://github.com/legoboyvdlp/A320-family-liveries
Commit 6673dc43d191163c1a4456b3bd7b00ace35f47f6
Original PNG: Liveries/IAE/8k/CCA-fuselage.png
Editable source: Sources/A320-200-IAE/Air China B-8490/CCA-fuselage.xcf
GPL-2. The repository license, contribution/readme terms, CCA.xml and original
XCF accompany the source in ThirdParty/AirChina-A320. The PNG is byte-for-byte
unchanged as Resources/Textures/AirChina-A320.png. Winglight uses this model's
native UV coordinates, retaining independent left/right layouts. The donor
livery represents B-8490, A320-232 / IAE / sharklets; Winglight currently uses
CFM56 / conventional wing fences. This is an adapted trial paint scheme, not
an exact replica of that registered airframe. CFM engine panel detail comes
from the original A320-family CFM texture; the IAE engine atlas is not used.

Winglight 1.3 modifications (2026-10-16): three-level body and two-level
nacelle curvature subdivision, welded geometric normals with independent UVs,
fixed aperture boundaries, and six cockpit panes generated from the 737
window frame boundary loops. The original CCA PNG is unchanged; the renderer
suppresses its obsolete painted windshield in the nose skin only, replacing
it with the separate 3D glass material. Generated glass joins mesh/BVH shadows.

Historical local livery catalog (110 model/livery combinations)
===================================================
A320-family-liveries, A320-family and 737-800YV supply the public catalog.
The former B737800-Liveries entries are excluded from publication because
that repository has no verified license grant. See THIRD_PARTY.md.
The retained images have per-file source URL,
fixed commit, SHA-256, original livery XML and licenses in ThirdParty/Liveries.
The catalog preserves the original airline/registration description. Some
paint schemes are historical or adapted from different engine/wingtip options.
See LIVERY_CATALOG.csv and scripts/fetch_liveries.py for the complete mapping.
The upstream repositories retain their paintkit/source projects; original
creator attribution and GPL license terms continue to apply.

A320 normal atlases (unmodified): Models/Effects/Lightmaps/Fuse-Main-Normal.png
and Engine-CFM56-Normal.png from the same pinned A320-family commit above.
Exact URLs and hashes are recorded in ThirdParty/A320/normal-maps.json.
